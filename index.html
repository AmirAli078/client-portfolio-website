About Advocate Toufeeque Ahemd Malik

Toufeeque Ahemd Malik ne LL.B University of Karachi se kiya aur 2011 se Sindh High Court mein practice kar rahe hain. Unka usool seedha hai: client ko case ki sahi position batana, phir us par poori tayari ke saath khara hona.

Wo Urdu, English aur Sindhi mein client ko guide karte hain, taa-ke har shakhs apna case khud samajh sake.
